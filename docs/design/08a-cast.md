## How names and genders were chosen
**Naming rules (Charles):**
1. No names that read as references to characters in other media or to real people.
2. No two names that sound alike: every cast member has a distinct initial, and no two share a rhythm or ending.
3. A roughly 50/50 gender split. Roles and characterizations were fixed first, then gender was assigned randomly.
4. Names fit Stardew's world with a quiet alchemical undertone.

Names also avoid vanilla and SVE NPC names (e.g. Robin, Maru, Marnie, Morgan, Victor, Sophia).

**Gender assignment:** two were fixed by Charles's direction (the Mentor is female; the Glassblower is Sandy's *nephew*). The other nine were shuffled with a cryptographic RNG into a split that makes 11 humans come out as near 50/50 as possible. The result is **6 female, 5 male**. The Familiars are spirits and have no gender.

**Trope check:** after assignment, one combination leaned on a stereotype: the Reagent Hunter came out male with a "scarred, gruff" characterization. Charles approved Ivo as written (2026-10-13). The rest read as counter-trope or neutral.

## Core three

### A. Oriel: the Mentor Alchemist
- **Who:** a wizard-turned-alchemist and the Wizard's former student. She mastered what he could teach, then went on to study subjects beyond his expertise. They remain on respectful terms, with a teacher's mild bemusement on his side.
- **Does:** appears in the **opening cutscene** (Town, 2 days after the CC opens, 6am–3pm). She leads the player behind the Community Center to the **derelict workshop** in its basement, and mentions **Tobin** as someone who could help get it running again. She later spends part of her week researching there.
- **Function:** the primary teacher of the **magical and ritual** side. She champions **grand rituals and dramatic single-batch processes** (doc 04), carries the Great Work storyline, and stays above the Joja moral debate, which Dr. Sorenson carries instead.

### B. Tobin: the Mechanist Engineer
- **Who:** a self-taught engineer who builds steam and clockwork apparatus; friendly with Clint and Robin. He isn't wary of magic: he believes engineering is the right approach *to* alchemy.
- **Does:** restores the workshop's machinery alongside the Curator, sells and upgrades mechanical apparatus, and teaches boilers, conveyors and Automate-friendly lines.
- **Function:** the voice of **automation and clever solutions**. He uses science to focus and direct magic predictably. The upside is scale and automation; the downside is that constrained magic can't reach the heights of wonder. He's the counterpoint to Oriel's grand rituals.

### C. Finch: the Travelling Merchant
- **Who:** a colourful trader with a wagon of exotic reagents and curios, on a fixed weekly route. The wagon stands somewhere out in the world, never in the workshop, at a spot that is open with and without SVE (Charles, 2026-10-20; doc 17 §7a).
- **Does:** sells rare reagents, buys surplus essences, and stocks a rotating "curiosity" that hints at an undiscovered recipe.
- **Function:** the economic valve. Relieves input bottlenecks (balance rule), gives money-making a channel within the caps, and offers discovery hooks through flavour text.

## Approved additions

### 1. Dr. Sorenson: the Joja Defector (economy, story)
- **Who:** a former JojaMart R&D chemist who quit after Joja tried to patent "synthetic essence"; now renting a room at the Saloon.
- **Does:** offers **bulk contracts** (standardized products at fixed prices) and, later, a branching quest about Joja's industrial alchemy.
- **Function:** ties ValleyOpus to Stardew's **community vs. industry** theme and carries the moral debate so Oriel can stay above it. Gives the Mechanist branch a counterweight and a market for bulk output.

### 2. Hesper: the Astrologer & Ley Surveyor (planetary weekdays, exploration, hints) · *merged with the former #3*
- **Who:** an eccentric stargazer and cartographer of "the valley's hidden currents". She **doesn't live in town**: she visits **3–4 days a week** and wanders through the day between the **Wizard's tower, the Library/Museum, the derelict workshop and an outdoor spot** (proposed: the Mountain lake or Cindersap Forest).
- **Does:** mails a weekly **almanac letter** (coming planetary days, favourable weather, celestial events), hosts night-time star rituals, reveals **ley nodes** on the map, sells survey tools, and teaches **arcane logistics** (sympathetic vessels and aether conduits).
- **Function:** makes the **planetary weekday** system legible without a wiki (the strongest in-world hint channel), turns exploration into an alchemy input through ley nodes, and guides the exploration capstone.

### 4. Ivo: the Reagent Hunter (combat)
- **Who:** a scarred Adventurer's Guild regular who harvests monster parts "for the gentlemen in the city"; gruff and practical. *(See the trope check above.)*
- **Does:** posts **monster-reagent bounties**, trades combat reagents, and teaches bombs, blade coatings and combat elixirs.
- **Function:** gives **combat** players a reason to engage and points them to the shadow substances (Mors, Tenebrae). Per the inventory rule, reagents come from **existing vanilla monster loot**, not new drops (doc 03b). Hosts the combat capstone.

### 5. Delta: the Tide-Pool Naturalist (fishing)
- **Who:** a marine biologist on a research stint near the tide pools, fascinated by "the chemistry of the sea". She and **Willy** clearly know each other from somewhere, but neither says how. It stays a mystery.
- **Does:** asks for specific fish and sea items, teaches Sal Marinum, Luna and Hydragenum uses, and opens fish-based Aqua/Sal routes.
- **Function:** gives **fishing** players a reason to engage and supplies non-crop Aqua/Sal sources (the anti-bottleneck rule).

### 6. Agnes: the Assayer (mining, transmutation)
- **Who:** a no-nonsense metallurgist hired by the Ministry of Mining to inspect the Quarry; skeptical of transmutation until proven wrong.
- **Does:** the player **meets her in Clint's shop**, which introduces the mining features. She assays and certifies metals, and buys the planetary metals (Argentum, Stannum, Plumbum). **Advanced transmutation and her Quarry crucible station unlock once the Quarry bridge is repaired** through base-game bundle progress (or the Joja equivalent).
- **Function:** gives **mining** an alchemy role and acts as the in-world validator of transmutation, so a successful transmutation becomes a story beat. Pairs with Clint.

### 7. Bennet: the Desert Glassblower (desert, apparatus crafting)
- **Who:** **Sandy's nephew**, an artisan who runs a kiln in the Calico Desert, where the sand is "perfect".
- **Does:** crafts glassware (alembics, retorts, pelicans) from Pulvis and Vitrum, and takes commissions to upgrade apparatus.
- **Function:** a reason to visit the desert, a satisfying crafting gate for advanced arcane apparatus, and a sidegrade source (different glassware shapes open different routes). The desert opens only after the bus repair, so starter glassware comes from the workshop (doc 02).

### 8. "The Curator" / Cyprian: the Workshop's Ghost (story, restoration bundles)
- **Who:** the gentle spirit of the workshop's original founder, bound to it by an unfinished Great Work. **His identity is a mystery at first.**
- **Does:** presents the **workshop restoration bundles** and **works alongside Tobin**. At first they butt heads over **progress vs. tradition**. Tobin's respect grows when he realizes the Curator is deeply qualified to discuss alchemical apparatus. The reveal: the Curator is **Cyprian, someone Tobin always looked up to but misunderstood**, told in a cutscene, never a letter (Charles, 2026-10-20). Each learns from the other.
- **Function:** the narrative engine for workshop restoration, an emotional arc for Tobin, and a Spiritus source. Gives the Great Work its payoff.

### 9. Lark: the Eager Apprentice (flavour, approachability)
- **Who:** an enthusiastic young cousin of one of the town families (Jodi's or Pam's; a deferred pass, doc 10) who idolizes the player's experiments.
- **Does:** copies recent discoveries with gently comic results, remarks on what the player hasn't tried yet, and occasionally gifts residue "projects".
- **Function:** a **discovery hint** system disguised as flavour. **Tone guard:** mischief stays small and warm, in Stardew's register (closer to Vincent and Jas than slapstick), and appears rarely.

### 10. The Four Familiars: Elemental Spirits (Laboratory, Mystic flavour)
- **Who:** a Salamander (Ignis), Undine (Aqua), Gnome (Terra) and Sylph (Aer): small spirits who bond with a Laboratory.
- **Does:** bonded to a **Tier 3** Laboratory to specialize it. **One familiar per Laboratory, no duplicates.** Each boosts the operations of its element in that Lab (Salamander: Calcination and decomposition; Undine: Dissolution and Fermentation; Sylph: Separation and Distillation; Gnome: Conjunction and Coagulation), has small heart events, and is bonded by a hand-made rite any player can perform (doc 18 §3–4).
- **Function:** gives the Laboratory a soul and makes passive Lab effects diegetic. It creates a **soft cap of 4 specialized Labs per farm**: a 5th or later Lab still works but has no familiar bonus. Details are in doc 18.
