## Tone (Charles, doc 01)
- **Dark in a "rated PG" way**, close to vanilla Stardew's own tone, which already has ghosts, zombies and skeletons. Dark alchemy should feel **spooky and ominous, never grotesque**: ghosts, skeletons, shadows and eerie lights are fine; **blood and gore are not**.
- **Humour is mostly gentle whimsy.** Some characters get occasionally **sassy in cutscenes**. **Slapstick is reserved for the characters it fits** particularly well (decided: Finch's misfiring curios and the odd Tobin machine hiccup, plus Lark's mishaps kept small and restrained; Oriel, Hesper and Cyprian stay dry).
- **The feeling to leave:** after a big alchemy day, the player feels accomplished, and often clever (doc 07).

## The forbidden arts (Charles, doc 01)
- **Living transmutation is for plants only.** Homunculi and transmutation of animals or people are **explicitly forbidden**, and **the Wizard enforces the ban**. In play, this means plant work (seed transmutation, the Radicula; doc 03a) is the legal edge of the Art.
- **Story use (proposal):** the Wizard states the ban when the player first makes Anima, with the gravity of someone who has seen it broken. The ban is why alchemy's dark side worries people (below), and why the Witch's teaching of the dark twins stays on the right side of it: putrefaction of matter, Shades from monsters that have already fallen, never the making or remaking of a living creature.
- **Immortality is aspiration, not mechanics.** Alchemists in the valley's lore chased it; the Lapis Philosophorum's legend promises it; Cyprian's unfinished Work is shadowed by it. No item or ritual grants it (doc 03a).

## Alchemy's reputation (Charles, doc 01)
- Alchemy is a **respected discipline**. Villagers treat a working alchemist like a skilled craftsperson.
- The public also remembers that **dark alchemical magic was once a real public-safety problem**, so there's **wariness when an alchemist moves into the esoteric**: the dark twins, Shades, rituals at night.
- **In play (proposal):** villager dialogue shifts mildly once the player has made dark twins or filled a Gloam Lantern, with curiosity from some (Abigail) and polite unease from others. It's flavour only, never a friendship penalty. **Decided (Charles):** the historical trouble is a **named fictional incident**, set elsewhere and decades ago, told through a few readables. It is **not** why the workshop fell into ruin, so Cyprian stays sympathetic. Its name and details must not contradict vanilla or SVE lore (see below).

## Storyline shape (approved ingredients)
Letters from key NPCs · new NPCs · the **derelict workshop** restored through **CC-style bundles** · cutscene events · tie-ins with the **Wizard** and the **Witch**.

## The derelict workshop (approved)
- **Where:** a **basement under the Community Center**, entered by walking **behind the CC building** (a new warp on the Town map).
- **What it is:** the mod's **unlock and bundle hub and research centre**, one map that fits on one screen, with each room an area of it (Charles, 2026-10-20; doc 14 §2). Key NPCs (Oriel, Tobin, the Curator, Hesper on visiting days) spend part of their time here. Restoring rooms through bundles unlocks apparatus blueprints, recipes and story.
- **What it isn't:** the player's production space. Players build and run apparatus on their farm and in Laboratories (doc 02). The workshop is where they come to **unlock** things.
- **Joja route:** if the player sides with Joja, the CC becomes a warehouse, but the basement workshop is unaffected and stays reachable from behind the building. Dr. Sorenson's Joja storyline gains extra weight on that route. Bundles here are separate from CC bundles, so both routes work.

## Arc
1. **The Encounter (Spring Y1):** the opening cutscene in Town (doc 02). **Oriel**, a wizard-turned-alchemist and the Wizard's former student, walks the player behind the Community Center to the derelict workshop. She explains what it was, gives a starter apparatus, and mentions **Tobin** as someone who could get the place running.
2. **Restoration (Spring–Summer):** Tobin arrives and starts on the machinery alongside the mysterious **Curator**. Their early clashes over progress vs. tradition play out in events. Each restored room unlocks apparatus and the Laboratory blueprint at Robin's.
3. **Two Ways of Working (Summer–Fall):** Oriel's grand rituals and Tobin's engineered lines give voice to the core design axis. Neither is wrong; each has a ceiling the other doesn't.
4. **Bright and Dark (Fall):** the **Wizard** teaches the bright celestial substances (Sol, Luna, Vitae, Spiritus); the **Witch** teaches putrefaction and the dark twins (Tenebrae, Mors, Perditio). The player chooses whom to visit first; both are reachable.
5. **The Curator Revealed (late Y1):** Tobin learns the Curator is **Cyprian**, the founder he idolized but misunderstood. Mutual respect follows. The reveal is always a cutscene, never a letter (Charles, 2026-10-20).
6. **The Great Work (Winter Y1 → Year 2 at a diligent pace):** Cyprian's unfinished Opus, the capstones (doc 02), and Dr. Sorenson's Joja questline in parallel.
7. **The Ending (the 1.0 finished state):** completing the Great Work concludes the story: Cyprian can finally rest (the anti-Flamel payoff from the lore research), the cast's arcs resolve, the player earns the capstone items, and the valley changes visibly (doc 02). Post-ending, 1.0 keeps heart events, codex depth and rare recipes; an endless long tail is 2.0.

## Guidelines for the Wizard and the Witch
- **Teachers, not vendors.** They reveal *concepts* and *places* (where Aether pools, how putrefaction works), never finished recipes, which preserves discovery.
- **Use their existing characterization.** The Wizard: aloof, scholarly, bright magic, his tower. He regards Oriel as a former student who wandered off his path, with pride he won't admit. The Witch: reclusive, dark, the swamp, void. Neither becomes chatty or omnipresent.
- **Light gating.** Wizard content follows vanilla friendship (respecting SVE's changed gates). Witch content comes through letters, her hut and a few events, since she isn't a normal friendable NPC in vanilla. **With Alecto the Witch installed** (a 1.0 tie-in, Charles), the Witch is a full NPC, and our Witch content routes through her: dialogue, gift tastes and events written for Alecto. Without that mod, she stays the unnamed vanilla Witch (decided).
- **The Wizard enforces the ban** on living transmutation (above).
- **They complement each other.** Bright versus dark substances map onto the two teachers.
- **The Witch's dark arts run on Shades**, gathered in Gloam Lanterns from monster deaths (doc 04). She teaches the lantern and treats it matter-of-factly; the tone stays a little spooky, never grim. This gives combat players a reason to visit her.

## Guidelines for new NPCs
- Each serves **a gameplay function** (teach, trade, hint or gate) *and* fits Stardew's tone.
- Each links to **at least one existing villager** (Wizard → Oriel; Clint/Robin → Tobin; Gus → Dr. Sorenson; Willy → Delta; Clint → Agnes; Sandy → Bennet; Jodi or Pam → Lark; the Guild's Marlon → Ivo).
- Each owns **one hint channel or content area**, so information isn't duplicated.
- Names and genders follow the rules in 08a.
- **Lore & Worldbuilding pillar (doc 01):** no real-world or borrowed names, and pop-culture or historical nods only in flavour text, never in dialogue. Example: Tobin can mention that alchemy "all started with golems, centuries ago" (golems generically, no named legend), and now better tools let alchemists advance the craft through technology.

## Lore additions (Charles, doc 01)
The mod may **add to** vanilla and SVE lore, carefully: **nothing may contradict an established fact or undercut an existing characterization.** New history attaches to our own places and people (the workshop, Cyprian, ley nodes), or fills gaps vanilla leaves open, never rewrites what's there. Each lore addition gets a check against the vanilla and SVE wikis before it's written into dialogue.

## Mod tie-ins for 1.0 (Charles, doc 01)
Beyond SVE, players of **Alecto the Witch**, **Ridgeside Village** and **East Scarp** should find fun alchemy tie-ins in 1.0: their items mapped as Prime sources, a few lines and gift tastes for their characters, and where it fits, a ley node or small event in their areas. Other mods' tie-ins come in 2.0.
