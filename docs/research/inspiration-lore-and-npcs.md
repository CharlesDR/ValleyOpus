Feeds [03 Essences](../design/03-essences-and-decomposition.md), [03a Substances](../design/03a-substances-catalog.md), [08 Narrative](../design/08-narrative-and-npcs.md) and [08a Cast](../design/08a-cast.md). Character names follow the final cast in 08a (Oriel, Tobin, Dr. Sorenson, Hesper, Cyprian, Lark).

Verification key: **(v)** checked against a source this session; **(bk)** background knowledge, not re-checked.

## Naming rule (Charles, 2026-10-13)
**No real-world names in the mod.** Real people, texts and places stay in this file as *inspiration* only. Anything the player sees gets a fictional name that carries the idea without naming its source. Generic alchemical terms (salamander, ouroboros, alembic, bain-marie as a common noun) are fine; proper names (Mary, Brand, Paracelsus, Prague, Rosenkreutz, Emerald Tablet) are not. The same applies to names from other media (see [inspiration-alchemy-media.md](inspiration-alchemy-media.md)).

**Reference rule (Charles, 18:10; now the Lore & Worldbuilding pillar in doc 01):** nods to pop culture or real historical figures belong only in **flavour text** (item descriptions, codex lore, readables), never in in-game dialogue or as explicit character references. So Tobin's golem line speaks of golems generically, and Cyprian is never framed in-game as any real figure.

## 1. Historical inspirations, and what they become in the mod

| Real inspiration (not used in-mod) | The story | In-mod form (fictional name) |
|---|---|---|
| **Mary the Jewess** (Alexandria, 1st–3rd c. CE) (v) | Credited with the water bath (bain-marie), a three-armed still and a sealed vapour heater, and the axiom "One becomes two, two becomes three, and out of the third comes the one as the fourth." | **Tempering Bath:** a gentle-heat arcane apparatus that preserves quality. **The Fourfold Saying:** an unattributed inscription over the workshop door (see §4). |
| **Hennig Brand** (Hamburg, 1669) (v) | Searching for the philosopher's stone, he boiled down ~50 buckets of urine and found a white solid that glowed green in the dark: "cold fire", later named phosphorus. | **Cold Fire**, a substance discovered by accident while chasing something grander (from bone or animal products, toned for Stardew). Story hook for **Dr. Sorenson**: Joja trying to patent Cold Fire. Optionally a comic Lark mishap. |
| **Paracelsus** (16th c.) (bk) | Framed the tria prima and wrote of the four elementals (salamander, undine, gnome, sylph). | Already used for the Primes and the **Four Familiars**. The elemental type words are generic and fine; each Familiar still gets its own fictional name. |
| **Nicolas Flamel** (14th c.) (bk) | Legend says he made the Stone and never died. | **Cyprian** inverts it: a founder who *didn't* finish the Work and can't leave. |
| **Robert Boyle / Isaac Newton** (17th c.) (bk) | Measured, sceptical experimenters who still pursued alchemy privately. | **Tobin's** temperament: rigorous and sceptical of mysticism, yet not anti-magic. |
| **John Dee** (16th c.) (bk) | Court astrologer and scholar of the heavens. | **Hesper's** scholarly-astrologer flavour. |
| **"Puffers"** (period slang) (bk) | Would-be alchemists who only worked the bellows and burned money. | A generic word, fine to use: the Merchant's teasing nickname for the player's early failures. |
| **Clay golems** (Jewish folklore, incl. the Prague legend) (bk) | A clay servant animated by a word. | **Tobin's historical footnote** (§3). No golem item; the inventory budget stays untouched. |

## 3. Tobin and the golems (Charles's direction)

A historical footnote in Tobin's dialogue, not an item:

> "It all started with golems, you know. Centuries ago, alchemists shaped clay and spoke a word, and it moved. Crude, but it worked. Now we have gears, pressure gauges, steam. Some of us think the Art can finally grow up."

Hooks this opens, all optional:
- **Tobin ↔ Cyprian.** Cyprian belongs to the old tradition Tobin is talking about. Their progress-vs-tradition clash has a concrete subject: was the golem a crude beginning, or a lost art? Cyprian's reveal can recast the golems as wiser than Tobin assumed.
- **Workshop readable:** a cracked clay figure on a shelf, inspectable with the Assayer's Lens for a lore line.
- **A Mechanist profession flavour line** referencing "the old clay-workers".

## 4. A story spine from the Fourfold Saying

"One becomes two, two becomes three, and out of the third comes the one as the fourth." Shown in-mod unattributed, carved over the workshop door.
- **One:** Oriel alone, in the opening cutscene.
- **Two:** Tobin arrives; the two halves of the Art.
- **Three:** the Wizard and the Witch add the bright and dark views.
- **The one as the fourth:** the player, who unites all of it in the Great Work.

## 5. Suggestions for the cast

- **Oriel:** carries a three-armed still and quotes the Fourfold Saying. As the Wizard's former student who went beyond him, she can also echo the "two rival magicians" idea from the media research, but as teacher and outgrown pupil rather than equals.
- **Tobin:** the golem footnote (§3) and the Boyle-style sceptic; a secret notebook of anomalies he can't explain is a good heart-event beat.
- **Dr. Sorenson:** the Cold Fire patent quest.
- **Cyprian:** the anti-Flamel; completing the Great Work lets him rest.
- **Hesper:** owns the Verdant Tablet and planetary-day lore.
- **Four Familiars:** elemental types from Paracelsus, each with its own fictional name.
